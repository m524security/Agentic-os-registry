# Dynamic Deployment
1. Parse goal and acceptance criteria.
2. Classify risk and permissions.
3. Query workflow/tool/skill/agent registries.
4. Choose minimum sufficient intelligence.
5. Construct temporary team and execution DAG.
6. Execute with checkpoints.
7. Verify independently.
8. Recover/replan on failure.
9. Synthesize result.
10. Record trace and candidate lessons.
